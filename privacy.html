Privacy Policy – Mr Muscle Health

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Mr Muscle Health is a personal tool used only by its owner.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, it reads read-only sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, respiratory rate, oxygen saturation, steps, calories and exercise data from the Google Health API.

How data is used and stored

The data is written only to a folder in the owner's own Google Drive, for the owner's personal health review. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the Drive folder.
